Three things this watches for at Malta: VistaJet arrivals and departures, anything flagged military by ICAO24 address (departures only), and commercial — everything else departing LMML. None of the three lists are exhaustive — see the note below.

Departure data from OpenSky Network (public ADS-B). Neither section is a complete picture: aircraft that aren't broadcasting ADS-B — including any military aircraft actively avoiding tracking — simply won't appear here at all.

VistaJet · possible repositioning flags a short gap between an aircraft's last arrival and a later departure - it only applies to departures, not arrivals. LMML is VistaJet's home base, so that's often just routine operations, not an empty leg. For a flight you can actually book, check the XO app directly.

Military flights are identified by matching each aircraft's ICAO24 address against tar1090-db, a community-maintained list of known-military address ranges. It's an inference from the address block, not an official designation, and it isn't country-specific — it flags military aircraft worldwide.

Commercial is just what's left after the other two categories are pulled out — in practice mostly scheduled airline traffic through LMML, but technically any departure that isn't flagged VistaJet or military lands here, including other private or general aviation. Kept for 3 days rather than 30, since there's simply a lot more of it.

New VistaJet or military hits can also trigger a WhatsApp message if that's set up - see the README. Commercial never does; there's too much of it.
